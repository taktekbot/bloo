# ✳ Bloo

**Preview STL assemblies in color.**

You shouldn't have to print a thing to find out what it looks like. Bloo assembles your STLs in the browser, snaps them together, and lets you try filament colors until one feels right. One HTML file, no install, nothing uploaded.

**Use it:** https://taktekhq.github.io/bloo/ — or just open `index.html`.

Drop in STL or OBJ files — or hit **Try the demo** on the start screen to load an example model without uploading anything of your own. The demo is the [Atomic Stack](https://www.printables.com/model/1784047-atomic-stack-mcm-modular-drawer-valet-system) by Thunder Butter Designs; all credit to the designer, and the printable files live on their post. (Demo files are served from [`demo/`](demo/) — see the readme there.)

If your files were exported in place, Bloo reassembles them exactly as the designer intended, seated on the floor. Drag parts to move them, shift-drag to box-select and move groups, edges snap magnetically to neighbors (Alt bypasses), Ctrl+Z undoes anything. Click a part, pick a filament, save a snapshot.

Built around the [Atomic Stack](https://www.printables.com/model/1784047-atomic-stack-mcm-modular-drawer-valet-system) by Thunder Butter Designs; works with any model.
